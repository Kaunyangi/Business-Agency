Motion — Terms & Conditions for Organizers & Hosts

1. Listing is free. Publishing an event or a stay property on Motion carries no upfront cost. Motion earns only from completed, paid bookings, via the service fee or commission disclosed at checkout.

2. Accuracy. You are responsible for the accuracy of everything you list — event details, ticket quantities, property details, room availability and pricing. Misleading listings may be removed.

3. Payouts. Funds from paid bookings settle to your Motion Pay wallet, net of the applicable fee/commission for that category, disclosed on every order.

4. Cancellations. You must honor confirmed bookings. Repeated cancellations or no-shows may result in account review.

5. Buyer data. Names, phone numbers, emails and ID numbers collected at checkout are used to issue tickets/vouchers and for support and compliance purposes only.

6. Fees are transparent. Any commission or service fee is computed automatically and shown as a line item before payment — never hidden.
